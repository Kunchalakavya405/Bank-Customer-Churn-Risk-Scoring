# Bank Customer Churn Risk Scoring

## Project title
**Predictive Modeling and Risk Scoring for Bank Customer Churn**

This project uses the supplied European bank customer dataset to predict whether a customer is likely to churn and to convert the prediction into an interpretable churn-risk score.

## Objectives
- Predict customer churn.
- Generate churn probability scores.
- Identify important churn drivers.
- Reduce false-positive churn detection.
- Improve model interpretability.
- Enable scenario-based customer risk analysis.
- Provide an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

## Dataset
`European_Bank.csv` contains 10,000 customer records and 14 columns. The target variable is `Exited`.

Important fields include:
- CreditScore
- Geography
- Gender
- Age
- Tenure
- Balance
- NumOfProducts
- HasCrCard
- IsActiveMember
- EstimatedSalary
- Exited

Customer ID and Surname are excluded from model training because they are identifiers rather than predictive business features.

## Methodology
1. Data inspection and quality checks.
2. Missing-value handling.
3. Removal of non-informative identifiers.
4. One-hot encoding of Geography and Gender.
5. Numerical feature scaling.
6. Feature engineering:
   - Balance-to-Salary ratio
   - Product density indicator
   - Product-engagement interaction
   - Age-tenure interaction
7. Stratified 80/20 train-test split.
8. Model comparison:
   - Logistic Regression
   - Decision Tree
   - Random Forest
   - Gradient Boosting
9. Evaluation using Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score and ROC-AUC.
10. Interactive churn-risk scoring through Streamlit.

## Model results
Results are stored in `model_metrics.csv`. For the supplied dataset and the included configuration, Gradient Boosting achieved the highest ROC-AUC among the tested models and is used by the application.
